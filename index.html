Questions

Will anything be sent without my approval?

No. Messages to you and the advisors you add go directly. Anything addressed outside your team shows you the exact message first and goes only when you say yes.

Is this legal or tax advice?

No. Lockwood is software. It gives estimates and prepares drafts for you and your own advisors. Your CPA or attorney reviews anything before it is signed or filed.

I already use a sales tax tool. Isn't that enough?

Sales tax tools help you collect going forward. A buyer looks back, and looks at more than sales tax: contractors, entity, deferred revenue, state income tax and 1099s. Lockwood covers all of it and prices what's already happened.

How do I get started?

Request a review on this page. We reply by email to confirm scope, then send a secure link for your records. Lockwood reads nothing until you agree to the terms.

Who checks the work?

Every quote is checked against the page it came from and every figure comes from the calculation, not from a guess, before you see it. Findings from scanned pages get a person's check first. You and your advisors decide what to do, and your CPA or attorney reviews anything filed or signed.

What do you need from me?

Sales or billing reports by customer location, two or three years of tax returns, your books, payroll reports, and contractor payments. PDF, Excel, CSV, Word, images and zip files are fine, and scanned pages are read too. Large files go through a secure upload link: up to 25 files, 40 MB each, 200 MB per upload. You can forward the link to your CPA.

I'm selling in a year or two. Is it too early?

Usually not. Most fixes, like registering and filing back returns through state programs, take months. Starting early gives you time and keeps the issues out of the negotiation.

What doesn't Lockwood do?

It doesn't prepare or file tax returns, file anything with an agency, represent you before an agency, negotiate, give legal or tax opinions, decide QSBS eligibility, audit, or do quality of earnings work. It also can't promise it found every issue. It covers the areas on this page and shows its sources so your advisors can check.

What happens to my documents?

They're encrypted for each engagement and used only for your work. Source files are deleted 30 days after delivery. We keep the report and work papers.
